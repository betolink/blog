Joshua Tree had been on my bucket list for a while. We finally went a month ago,
kept the visit short, and packed in as much desert as we could.

I’ve always liked deserts, and seeing petroglyphs there makes me think that people have 
been reading this landscape and its skies for a very long time. The night skies were
impressive even without a new moon. I got to use my Dwarf 3 telescope again and captured 
Barnard 33, the Horsehead Nebula. 🌵🌌

{{< gallery >}}

On the way back we made an express visit to the Palm Springs Art Museum. I ran into
Alexander Calder's *Big Red* (1959), and it reminded me of the
cover of [Introduction to Algorithms](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262046305/introduction-to-algorithms/) turns out it is the same Artist.
